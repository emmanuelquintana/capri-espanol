# Factions

## Megacorporations

- **Arasaka.** Japanese megacorp: security, banking, manufacturing. Run by the Arasaka family: Saburo (founder,
  "Emperor"), sons Kei (dead) and Yorinobu, daughter Hanako (Kiji faction, netrunner). HQ: Arasaka Tower, Corpo Plaza,
  rebuilt after Johnny Silverhand's 2023 nuke. Sponsors the Arasaka Academy, runs Mikoshi and Soulkiller, owns the Relic.
  Black-clad guards, Adam Smasher, counter-intel, Arasaka Industrial Park (Santo Domingo). Capri was built here.
- **Militech.** US arms and PMC giant; Arasaka's main rival; supplies the NUSA military. Moved on Night City in 2077
  (see Panam Palmer's Aldecaldos storyline). In Edgerunners S1, suspected of sponsoring Maine's crew.
- **Kang Tao.** Chinese smart-gun maker (guided caseless ammo, "smart" pistols). Kiril Yang works here. Sells
  weapons across Asia and the US; expanding into mercenary and security markets.
- **Biotechnica.** Biotech/agri: CHOOH2 fuel crop, protein farms south of Night City.
- **Trauma Team International.** Premium rapid-response medics; billing starts at the call. Partners with Arasaka,
  Militech, Night Corp, Kiroshi, others.
- **Others.** Petrochem, EBM, Night Corp, Kiroshi Opticals, Zetatech, Rocklin Augmentics, Eurodyne (music), NetWatch
  (guards the Blackwall).
- **Corporate Wars.** Arasaka vs Militech; the Fourth Corporate War (early 2020s) hit Night City; the 2023 Tower nuke
  ended the worst of it. The Unification War (2070) split Night City from California.

## Law and order

- **NCPD.** Night City Police Department. Underfunded, overstretched, contracts out to corps; they cannot or will not
  enter many neighborhoods. Beat cops, Special Investigations Unit, Cyberpsycho Sighting hotline (V can "scan" targets).
- **Max-Tac.** NCPD's elite anti-cyberpsycho strike team, heavy armor and heavy chrome. Called when a cyberpsycho goes
  too far. V can see them in the game; whether they matter in Edgerunners S1 is thin.
- **Trauma Team.** Armed medics in white and red AVs.
- **Corporate security.** Arasaka and Militech run their own enforcers; corp territory is private law.

## Nomads

Clans that left corporate life for the road. Strong family bonds, cars, rural contracting, smuggling, infrastructure
(ConAg, maglev). **Seven Nations** include the **Aldecaldos** (Saul Bright, Panam Palmer; salvage and smuggling) and
**Snake Nation** (largest, decentralised alliance of independents; D's old clan). In Edgerunners 2 an Arasaka-orchestrated
Maelstrom attack wipes out D's Snake Nation clan.

## Pro roles

Solo, Netrunner, Techie, Fixer, Ripperdoc, Rockerboy, Corpo, Nomad, Media, Lawman. Fixers are the brokers with the
money (Rogue, Dex, Wakako, Faraday); they carry the credit and the blame.
