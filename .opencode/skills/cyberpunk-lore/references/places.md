# Places

## Night City

Free city-state on Del Coronado Bay, Pacific coast, between North and South California. Founded 1990s by Richard
Night as Coronado City; renamed 1998; separated from California 2070. Slogan "City of Dreams". Six districts plus
the Badlands around.

### City Center
- **Corpo Plaza.** Arasaka Tower, Militech and other corp towers. **Arasaka Tower**: HQ, rebuilt after 2023 nuke;
  Saburo's office near the top; Mikoshi access. Climax of 2077; Arasaka Academy is also in City Center.
- **Downtown**, **Memorial Park**, **Embers** (high-end club), **Konpeki Plaza** (luxury hotel; V and Jackie's
  Konpeki heist).

### Watson
- **Kabuki** (market and slums; Roman Carax's home, Lizzie's Bar (Mox), and in S1 Hotel Raito, Yaiba Tower, Kiwi's
  apartment), **Little China**, **Northside** (industrial, Maelstrom turf; S1: Pilar and Rebecca's apartment, All
  Foods Factory, **Totentanz** the Maelstrom club in an abandoned hotel), **Arasaka Waterfront**.
- **Afterlife.** Famous bar at Little China, run by Rogue Amendiares. Mercs hang here; a "Legend" gets their name
  on the wall (and a drink is named after them). The reference hangout for mercs and gigs. Tom's Diner is another
  Watson spot in S1.
- Megabuilding H10, Jig-Jig Street, Misty's Esoterica, Viktor's clinic.

### Westbrook
- **Japantown** (Tyger Claws; S1: David and Lucy's penthouse, Lucy's apartment), **North Oak** (rich, celebrity
  houses; the Columbarium where Yupi's father rests), **Charter Hill**.

### Heywood
- **The Glen**, **Vista del Rey**, **Wellsprings** (Valentino barrios), **Jacked and Coke** (S1 bar), **Bulwark**
  (S1). Latino neighborhoods plus a rich strip.

### Santo Domingo
- **Arroyo**, **Rancho Coronado** (S1: Aldo's place), **Arasaka Industrial Park**, factories and megabuildings.
  David Martinez's home is Megabuilding H4; Doc's clinic is here too. Corps use it as a test bed.

### Pacifica
- Failed resort district. **Coastview**, **West Wind Estate**, **Dogtown** (walled combat zone, Phantom Liberty,
  ruled by Kurt Hansen with Barghest). Voodoo Boys' Pyramid Song.

### The Badlands
Desert and hills around the city: nomad camps (Aldecaldos), Wraiths, Biotechnica protein farms in the south.
Edgerunners S1: David and the crew go east. The Eastern Wastelands.

## Elsewhere

- **The Moon.** Lucy's dream; in S1 she reaches it after David's death. Tourist shuttle from Night City.
- **The Net.** Old Net (haunted by rogue AIs), cut off by the Blackwall. Mikoshi sits in Arasaka-controlled cyberspace.
- **Mikoshi.** Arasaka's soul-storage fortress; orbital servers, access points in Arasaka facilities.
- **Arasaka Industrial Park.** Capri's biochip was stolen from here by King's Crew (Edgerunners 2, episode 1).
