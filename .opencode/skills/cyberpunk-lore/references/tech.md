# Tech, chrome, and the Net

## Cyberware (chrome)

| Chrome | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sandevistan** | Spinal-mounted "operating system": time dilation. The user moves in slow-motion world; leaves afterimage trails. Heavy strain. David's is military grade; Adam Smasher has an experimental one |
| **Kerenzikov** | Reflex booster; brief slow-down on aiming/dodging, lighter than a Sandevistan |
| **Mantis Blades** | Retractable arm blades |
| **Gorilla Arms** | Reinforced cyberarms for crushing strength; Maine, Dorio, Rebecca, Omi |
| **Monowire** | Monofilament whip in the fingertips; also cuts through Net-connected targets |
| **Projectile Launch System (PLS)** | Wrist launcher for grenades and darts |
| **Cyberdeck / Neuroport** | Gear that lets a netrunner jack in; the port is in the skull |
| **Cyberoptics (Kiroshi)** | Eye replacements: zoom, thermal, scan |
| **Second Heart** | Backup heart; revives the user once |
| **Optical Camo** | Cloaking skin |
| **Subdermal armor, Pain Editor, EMP Threading, Self-ICE, Biomonitor, Cyberlegs / Jumpboosters** | Standard mercenary gear |
| **Cyberskeleton** (S1) | Arasaka experimental exoskeleton plan; Tanaka's plan was to test it on David |
| **Relic** | Arasaka biochip that stores digitized engrams (psyches); see Soulkiller |

## Cyberpsychosis

Too much chrome without enough "humanity" breaks the mind: violence, paranoia, hallucinations. Maelstrom courts it.
In Edgerunners S1: Maine, David (fatally close), and others. In Edgerunners 2: King. People with high tolerance
(David Martinez, D, Talia) can take more chrome than normal; ripperdocs prescribe suppressants and warn them.
Counter: moderation, meds, a clinic, or Max-Tac.

## Netrunning

- Netrunners "jack in" via a cyberdeck and neuroport to hack systems, enemies, and braindances.
- **ICE** (Intrusion Countermeasures): defense software. **Breach protocol** is the 2077 hacking minigame. **Quickhacks**
  are short offensive hacks on a target's cyberware.
- **The Net** is the global network; the old Net was wrecked by Rache Bartmoss's **DataKrash** (2022); it is now a
  haunted ruin of rogue AIs, walled off behind the **Blackwall** by NetWatch.
- **Old Net:** the child-netrunner program that Arasaka ran on Lucy and others; one of S1's tragedies.
- **Soulkiller:** a program by Alt Cunningham that digitizes a mind into an engram. Kills the original. Stolen by
  Arasaka; turned into an AI in Mikoshi.
- **Mikoshi:** Arasaka's soul-vault in orbit and cyberspace.

## Braindance (BD)

Records and replays another person's full sensory experience. Invented 2007 for therapy, now mainstream entertainment
(and illegal "XBDs" - extreme, crime, or snuff). Editing needs a braindance editor (Judy in 2077; Jimmy Kurosaki in S1;
Jessie at Sal Bigelow's Braindance Paradise in Edgerunners 2). Roman Carax shuns BD for old film.

## Money and daily life

- **Eddies**: eurodollars (E$). Gigs pay through fixers.
- **Delamain**: autonomous cab AI (2077). Cars: Quadra, Mizutani, Porsche, Thorton, Rayfield.
- **Corpo hierarchy**: executives, security, the "white collar" with the gadgets, fighting each other with proxies.
- **Cyberware clinics**: ripperdocs install; black-market ones are cheap and risky; Trauma Team revives members.
