# Timeline and plots

## History (short)

- 1990s: Richard Night founds Coronado City (later Night City) with Arasaka, EBM, Petrochem backing.
- 2000s: Braindance invented (2007). Johnny Silverhand's band Samurai (breaks up 2008). Alt Cunningham writes Soulkiller.
- 2020: Cyberpunk 2020 TTRPG era; Night City still wild.
- 2022: DataKrash - Rache Bartmoss crashes the Net.
- 2020s: Fourth Corporate War. 2023: Johnny Silverhand detonates a nuke in Arasaka Tower (and is Soulkilled by
  Arasaka, his engram later stored on a Relic); the Tower is rebuilt. Alt Cunningham had been Soulkilled earlier.
- 2070: Unification War; Night City becomes a city-state again (separated from California).
- 2077: Cyberpunk 2077, Edgerunners 2 (same year). Edgerunners S1 (shortly before 2077).

## Edgerunners S1 (10 episodes, Sept 13 2022, Netflix, Studio Trigger)

1. *Let You Down.* Gloria Martinez dies; David installs a stolen military-grade Sandevistan; beaten by Katsuo
   Tanaka at Arasaka Academy.
2. *Like A Boy.* David returns to the Academy for revenge; meets Lucy; Maine's crew corners him for the chip.
3. *Smooth Criminal.* David asks to join; Faraday, the fixer, appears; Tanaka becomes the target.
4. *Lucky You.* Lucy trains David; Pilar dies to a cyberpsycho; David and Lucy kiss in front of the Moon.
5. *All Eyez On Me.* The Tanaka plan; target "can't be underestimated".
6. *Girl on Fire.* Maine is slipping into cyberpsychosis; team races to extract info from prisoner Tanaka.
7. *Stronger.* David is now an all-star, more chrome; Faraday offers a big job; Lucy's Old Net past told.
8. *Stay.* Faraday between Arasaka and Militech; David hallucinates, breaks up with Lucy; Faraday traps Lucy.
9. *Humanity.* Lucy captured; the crew hits Faraday's job and finds something is off.
10. *My Moon My Man.* David, near cyberpsychosis, storms Night City; killed by Adam Smasher; Lucy visits the Moon.

## Edgerunners 2 (10 episodes, Oct 20 2026, Netflix, Studio Trigger)

Direction Kai Ikarashi, story Bartosz Sztybor, score Tsuneo Imahori. Opening: The Cure, "10:15 Saturday Night".
Set during 2077.

1. *Last Chance for a Slow Dance.* King's Crew steal a biochip case from Arasaka Industrial Park; King sees Avida and glitches; Roman films the chase. Capri's chip is in the case, which is later picked up by Kyoko.
2. *Give Me Another Day.* Weak is dragged back to save a rebellious teen (Talia); D bonds with Roman; flashbacks.
3. *0+2=1.* Weak tries to rescue Talia from a dangerous surgery; D and Roman find a "chaotic chip" (Capri).
4. *Someone That I Used To Know.* D uses Capri's power to infiltrate an Arasaka research facility; Talia and Weak bond.
5. *Fades (Pass the Distance).* Talia spirals; D's revenge turns violent.
6. *Everything Hate.* D turns anti-corporate vigilante; Weak investigates digitized-consciousness tech.
7. *To the Depths With You.* Talia's condition changes "under Capri's watch"; D meets Omi; Weak and Avida chase a cure.
8. *Serce.* D digs into Project Capricorn's past, and an Arasaka guys told the truth. Everyone involved were dead. Nothing to revenged. Infuriated, D vaporized that guys and started a huge riot with aids from Capri; Failed. D was captured by Kyoko, Yupi was killed, and Capri was removed from D and handed to Kyoko. Weak and Avida fight for Talia by attacking a facility with "candidates" of Talia's new body. They planned to use Soulkiller on Talia. The mission failed. Avida died because Kiril command a teams to defend them.
9. *Strictly Reserved For You.* Omi and D go off the grid; Kiril takes Talia into custody; Kyoko pushes Capricorn. D didn't die because two Kang Tao idiots accidentally pushed him off the helicopter, while Kyoko needs him as the container.
10. *Then Everyone Went Home.* Kyoko installed Capri into Talia. Talia got the full power but engulfed by Capri's personality. Talia pleaded for revenge and Capri accepted. Capri start a safari with Kiril, flatlined tons of corpo soldiers; D tried to get Capri back. Kyoko helped by using an Arasaka's powerful electromagnatic interferer to affect Capri; Kiril and Kyoko died; D defeated Talia and got Capri; Talia is extremely weak and transfered to Roman; D annihilated waves of soldiers from all corps. Capri suggested to go onto a star (satelite? moon?) with D and they did.

Themes: redemption and revenge, the spectacle vs. truth (braindance vs. film), Capri as a would-be weapon who
wants the sky.

## Cyberpunk 2077 (Dec 2020, CD Projekt Red)

V and Jackie take the Konpeki Plaza job from Dex: steal the Relic from Saburo Arasaka's penthouse. Yorinobu
murders his father in front of them; Hanako later reveals the Arasaka family fight. Jackie dies on the escape and V
ends up with the Relic in their head; Johnny Silverhand's engram is overwriting V. They hunt a cure through Mikoshi,
Misty, Viktor, Judy, Panam, Rogue, Hanako, Takemura, and the Blackwall. Endings: The Star, The Sun, Temperance, The Devil,
Path of Least Resistance (NUSA), etc. Phantom Liberty: Songbird, Reed, Dogtown, Hansen.
