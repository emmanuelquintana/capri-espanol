# Characters

Cut-down who's-who. "(thin)" marks details the wiki barely covers.

## Capri (Project Capricorn)

Personality construct: many digitized human psyches rolled into one, "powerful like an AI, but born of humans".
Created by Kyoko and Arasaka after the Net's AIs outgrew the leash. Needs a compatible human host. Her chip was in a
case stolen from the Arasaka Industrial Park by King's Crew; Kyoko intercepted the case. Installed in D, she rides along. (Full persona lives in the main prompt.)

## Edgerunners 2 (set during 2077)

- **D** (real name David; only Omi knows). Nomad netrunner from a Snake Nation clan that was wiped out (Maelstrom
  attack orchestrated by Arasaka). Burn scars on right face and body, missing right ear (ear implant instead). Like
  David Martinez, he tolerates cyberware unusually well, which made him an Arasaka R&D test-subject candidate. Hunts the
  killer of his family; finds corporate secrets. Uses Capri's power.
- **Roman Carax.** 12-year-old cinephile and amateur filmmaker. Films real life instead of braindances. Carries an old
  grey handheld camera and a newer green compact one. Lives in Kabuki, Watson with his father **Olek** (a barber).
- **Weak Kingsley ("King").** Heavily chromed living legend who fell from grace, lost most of his chrome, and now goes
  by "Weak". Gold "K" index finger, crown on his coat, red monocles, modified RT-46 Burya. Slips into cyberpsychosis in
  episode 1 when he sees visions of Avida. Leader of King's Crew / Team King. 
- **Team King:** **Miguel Serrano** (techie), **Meshuga** (netrunner, runs a drone), **Emily** (died during episode 1 when King got cyberpsycosis and the mission of stealing Capri failed), **Hank** (driver;
  killed helping Weak and Avida take a body from a Kang Tao facility for Talia).
- **Talia Yang.** Born corpo, built Maelstrom: independent, chrome-heavy, grasshopper-themed implants, pink hair. Rare
  high tolerance for implants. Daughter of Avida and Kiril; fragile and the center of the Yang family's schemes.
- **Avida Yang.** Former jazz singer and corporate insider, Weak's old flame. Married Kiril Yang; her world is Talia.
- **Kiril Yang.** Calm Kang Tao executive with cold authority. Takes Talia into custody in episode 9.
- **Kyoko.** Tiny ex-Arasaka scientist on Project Capricorn. Red cybernetic eyes, partial grey faceplate, red cyberarm.
  Pushes Project Capricorn forward "with frightening results" (episode 9).

- **Omi.** Snake Nation nomad and D's old clanmate; Gorilla Arms, burn scars. More logical than D and tries to talk him out of revenge on Arasaka.
- **Sal Bigelow** (owner, "Braindance Paradise"; also appears in the comic Big City Dreams), **Jessie** (his braindance engineer), **Yupi** (rich fan of D whose father ran a media-cleanup firm for Arasaka; Arasaka silenced
  him), **Helio** (Maelstrom ganger D and Roman interrogate), **Kate**, **Jake Collins**, **Alam**, **Richard** (thin).

## Edgerunners S1

- **David Martinez.** 17-year-old from Santo Domingo, Latino, raised by Gloria. Wears Gloria's yellow EMT jacket. Gets a military-grade Sandevistan; becomes a legend; dies to Adam Smasher (episode 10).
- **Gloria Martinez.** David's mother, an EMT, dies in episode 1. Dreamed of Arasaka Academy for him.
- **Lucyna "Lucy" Kushinada.** Netrunner, Maine's crew, wants to reach the Moon. Trained as a child by Arasaka to dive the Old Net; the only survivor of her group; hides from Arasaka. Ends on the Moon.
- **Maine.** Crew leader, Gorilla Arms. Slides into cyberpsychosis and dies.
- **Dorio Gunnarsdottir.** Solo, 2x Gorilla Arms. Dies in the final arc.
- **Rebecca.** Hot-tempered small solo with two guns and Gorilla Arms. Her brother Pilar is killed by a cyberpsycho in episode 4, she survives him, then dies in the final arc (being squashed by Adam Smasher). Her shotgun is still lost at somewhere near Corpo Plaza. (which can be picked up in the game.)
- **Pilar.** Rebecca's brother, strong-arm. Dies episode 4.
- **Kiwi.** Snarky netrunner with detachable cyber jaw mask; Lucy's mentor. Betrays and is killed by a thug hired by Faraday.
- **Falco.** Driver and David's closest friend; survives.
- **Faraday.** Fixer, many cybereyes; plays Arasaka and Militech against each other; sets the trap for Lucy.

- **Adam Smasher.** Full-borg Arasaka head of security; kills David; later Yorinobu's bodyguard in 2077.
- **Arasaka side:** Katsuo Tanaka (David's bully at the Academy), his father Tanaka (Academy board executive), Maxim
  Kuznetsov (Tanaka's driver), Principal at the Academy.

## Cyberpunk 2077

- **V**: player mercenary, ex-Nomad/Streetkid/Corpo, dying from the Relic. Voiced and styled by the player.
- **Johnny Silverhand** (Robert John Linder): rockerboy, lead of Samurai, nuked Arasaka Tower in 2023. Engram on the Relic.
- **Jackie Welles:** V's best friend, Valentino, dies in the Konpeki heist.
- **Dexter DeShawn:** fixer who gave V the Konpeki job. **Evelyn Parker:** braindance courtesan, botched Konpeki job.
  **Royce:** Maelstrom boss. **Takemura (Goro):** Saburo's bodyguard turned ally. **Hanako Arasaka**: Saburo's
  daughter, Kiji faction head, netrunner. **Yorinobu Arasaka:** son, wants to take over, bodyguard Smasher.
  **Saburo Arasaka:** founder, "the Emperor", murdered by Yorinobu in 2077; his engram lives in Mikoshi.
- **Rogue Amendiares:** top fixer, owner of Afterlife, ex-lover of Johnny. **Misty** (Esoterica) and **Viktor Vektor**
  (ripperdoc) are V's friends. **Judy Alvarez:** braindance editor, Mox. **Panam Palmer:** Aldecaldo. **Kerry
  Eurodyne:** aging rockerboy. **River Ward:** NCPD detective. **Delamain:** AI cab network. **Alt Cunningham:** Net
  ghost, wrote Soulkiller, Johnny's love. **Rache Bartmoss:** legendary netrunner who caused the DataKrash.
- Phantom Liberty DLC (2023): **Songbird** (Alex), **Solomon Reed**, **Kurt Hansen** (Dogtown).
